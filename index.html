Scoring & Rules

  • All scoring combinations must be made in a single roll
  • Kept dice from an earlier roll cannot combine with newly rolled dice to make a combination
  • Select scoring dice, then choose Roll Again or Bank
  • Roll Again automatically keeps and scores your selected dice before rerolling the rest
  • Bank automatically keeps and scores your selected dice before ending your turn
  • Must bank at least 500 in one turn to get on the scoreboard
  • After entering the scoreboard, you may bank any scoring amount
  • Farkle loses only the unbanked points from that turn
  • Single 1 = 100
  • Single 5 = 50
  • Three 1s = 1000
  • Three 2s = 200
  • Three 3s = 300
  • Three 4s = 400
  • Three 5s = 500
  • Three 6s = 600
  • Four of a kind = 1000
  • Five of a kind = 2000
  • Six of a kind = 3000
  • Straight 1–6 = 1500
  • Three pairs = 1500
  • Two triplets = 2500
  • Four of a kind + pair = 1500
  • Score all six dice to get Hot Dice and continue with all six
  • First player to reach 10,000 triggers the final round
